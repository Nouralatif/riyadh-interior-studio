Redefining Spaces Across the Heart of the Kingdom

Founded in Riyadh, Riyadh Interior Studio blends authentic Saudi architectural heritage with sleek, contemporary design. From warm, welcoming family Majlis lounges in Diriyah and Al Nakheel to high-impact executive offices in the King Abdullah Financial District (KAFD), we transform blank interiors into functional works of art.

Inspired by the warm desert limestone of Tuwaiq, traditional Najdi geometric patterns, and sustainable modern lighting, our designs honor Saudi hospitality while embracing innovation.
